Buildroot is licensed primarily under GPL-2.0-or-later with per-file and
package exceptions.

BusierBox uses Buildroot as a target build system. Buildroot package recipes can
fetch and build software under many licenses; selected payload packages keep
their own upstream license and notice obligations.

Pinned Buildroot source metadata is recorded in `manifests/sources.lock.json`.
Downloaded source archives are cached under `dl/` for offline rebuilds.

Upstream project:
https://buildroot.org/
